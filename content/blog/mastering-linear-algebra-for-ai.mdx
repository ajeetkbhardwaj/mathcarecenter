Modern machine learning models—from neural networks to Transformer LLMs—are fundamentally linear algebra operations interspersed with non-linear activation functions.

## Attention as Matrix Multiplication

The Scaled Dot-Product Attention mechanism in Transformers is defined as:

$$
\operatorname{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \operatorname{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V
$$

- $QK^\top$ computes pairwise similarity scores between Query vectors and Key vectors.
- Scaling by $\sqrt{d_k}$ prevents variance explosion in higher dimensions.
- Softmax converts scores into probability weights.
- Multiplying by $V$ computes a weighted linear combination of Value vectors.

## Loss Surface Curvature & The Hessian

Near a local minimum, a loss function $L(\theta)$ can be approximated by its second-order Taylor expansion:

$$
L(\theta + \delta) \approx L(\theta) + \frac{1}{2} \delta^\top H \delta
$$

where $H = \nabla^2 L$ is the symmetric Hessian matrix. The eigenvalues of $H$ dictate the steepness and direction of curvature along the loss surface.
