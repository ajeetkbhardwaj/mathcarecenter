Reading mathematics is non-linear. A single paper can take hours or days to digest fully. A structured 3-pass approach helps prevent getting stuck in notation.

## Pass 1: The High-Level Overview (15–20 minutes)
- Read the title, abstract, and introduction.
- Look at main theorem statements, ignoring proofs.
- **Goal**: Understand what problem the paper solves and why it matters.

## Pass 2: The Structural Skeleton (1–2 hours)
- Read the key definitions carefully.
- Trace the sequence of lemmas and proposition dependencies that lead to the main theorem.
- Note any steps or assumptions you don't immediately see.

## Pass 3: Verification & Re-derivation (Deep Dive)
- Work out proofs on paper line by line.
- Test edge cases or counterexamples where hypotheses are dropped.
- **Goal**: Master the core technique so you can apply it to your own work.
