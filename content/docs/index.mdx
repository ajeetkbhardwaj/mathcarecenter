Welcome to **Math Care Center**. This platform is designed to provide clear, rigorous, and visually grounded mathematics education.

## Core Learning Pillars

1. **Geometric Intuition**: Understand the underlying visual concepts—slopes, areas, linear transformations, and curvatures.
2. **Algebraic Rigour**: Precise definitions, conditions, and line-by-line proofs.
3. **Applied Computation**: Real-world application to physics, optimization, data science, and artificial intelligence.

## Learning Roadmap

Mathematics is built sequentially. Here is the recommended path through our courses:

- **Stage 1: Calculus** — Limits, derivatives, integrals, and vector calculus (Green's, Divergence, and Stokes' theorems).
- **Stage 2: Linear Algebra** — Vector spaces, linear transformations, determinants, spectral theory, and Singular Value Decomposition (SVD).
- **Stage 3: Modern Algebra** — Groups, cosets, Lagrange's theorem, normal subgroups, ring homomorphisms, and field extensions.
- **Stage 4: Probability** — Kolmogorov axioms, Bayes' theorem, PDF/CDF integrations, the Central Limit Theorem, and Markov chains.
