To study mathematics effectively, follow a logical progression where each topic builds on firm prerequisites.

## Recommended Course Sequence

### 1. Calculus
- **Prerequisites**: High school algebra and trigonometry.
- **Topics Covered**: $\varepsilon$–$\delta$ limits, differentiation, integration, multivariable calculus, and Stokes' theorem.
- **Why It Matters**: Calculus is the language of continuous change and optimization.

### 2. Linear Algebra
- **Prerequisites**: Basic calculus.
- **Topics Covered**: Vector spaces, linear maps, matrices, determinants, eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and SVD.
- **Why It Matters**: Linear algebra provides coordinates for multi-dimensional spaces and powers modern AI.

### 3. Modern Algebra
- **Prerequisites**: Proof familiarity and linear algebra.
- **Topics Covered**: Groups, subgroups, Lagrange's theorem, normal subgroups, rings, and fields.
- **Why It Matters**: Modern algebra reveals the deep symmetries and abstract structures in mathematics.

### 4. Probability & Stochastic Systems
- **Prerequisites**: Multivariable calculus and linear algebra.
- **Topics Covered**: Axiomatic probability, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, Central Limit Theorem, and Markov chains.
- **Why It Matters**: Probability models uncertainty, statistics, and randomized algorithms.
