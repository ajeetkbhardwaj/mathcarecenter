## Derivatives

| Function $f(x)$ | Derivative $f'(x)$ |
| --- | --- |
| $x^n$ | $n x^{n-1}$ |
| $e^x$ | $e^x$ |
| $\ln x$ | $\dfrac{1}{x}$ |
| $\sin x$ | $\cos x$ |
| $\cos x$ | $-\sin x$ |
| $\arctan x$ | $\dfrac{1}{1+x^2}$ |

**Rules**:
- Product Rule: $(uv)' = u'v + uv'$
- Quotient Rule: $\left(\frac{u}{v}\right)' = \frac{u'v - uv'}{v^2}$
- Chain Rule: $(f \circ g)'(x) = f'(g(x))g'(x)$

## Integrals

$$
\int x^n \,dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C \quad (n \neq -1), \qquad \int \frac{dx}{x} = \ln|x| + C
$$

$$
\int e^{ax}\,dx = \frac{e^{ax}}{a} + C, \qquad \int \frac{dx}{1+x^2} = \arctan x + C
$$

**Integration by Parts**:
$$
\int u\,dv = uv - \int v\,du
$$

## Taylor Series

$$
e^x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!} = 1 + x + \frac{x^2}{2!} + \frac{x^3}{3!} + \cdots
$$

$$
\sin x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!} = x - \frac{x^3}{3!} + \frac{x^5}{5!} - \cdots
$$

$$
\cos x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n x^{2n}}{(2n)!} = 1 - \frac{x^2}{2!} + \frac{x^4}{4!} - \cdots
$$

## Vector Calculus Operators

With Del operator $\nabla = \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\right)$:

- **Gradient**: $\nabla f$ (vector field of steepest growth)
- **Divergence**: $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F}$ (scalar flux density)
- **Curl**: $\nabla \times \mathbf{F}$ (vector rotational density)
- **Laplacian**: $\nabla^2 f = \nabla \cdot \nabla f$ (curvature / diffusion)

## Matrix Identities

$$
\det(AB) = \det(A)\det(B), \qquad \operatorname{tr}(AB) = \operatorname{tr}(BA)
$$

$$
(AB)^{-1} = B^{-1}A^{-1}, \qquad \operatorname{rank}(A) + \dim\ker(A) = n \quad \text{(Rank-Nullity)}
$$
