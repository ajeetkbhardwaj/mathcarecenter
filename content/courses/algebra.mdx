## Groups

A group $(G, \cdot)$ is a set $G$ with a binary operation satisfying associativity, existence of an identity element $e$, and existence of inverse elements $g^{-1}$ for all $g \in G$.

## Lagrange's Theorem

If $G$ is a finite group and $H \le G$ is a subgroup, then the order of $H$ divides the order of $G$:

$$
|G| = [G : H] \cdot |H|
$$

where $[G : H]$ is the number of left cosets of $H$ in $G$.

## Normal Subgroups & Quotient Groups

A subgroup $N \le G$ is normal ($N \trianglelefteq G$) if $g N g^{-1} = N$ for all $g \in G$.

When $N$ is normal, the set of cosets $G/N$ forms a group under $(aN)(bN) = (ab)N$, called the quotient group.

## Ring & Field Homomorphisms

A ring homomorphism $\varphi: R \to S$ preserves addition and multiplication. The First Isomorphism Theorem states:

$$
R / \ker(\varphi) \cong \operatorname{im}(\varphi)
$$
